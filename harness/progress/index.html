The pieces

Each piece is built and judged on its own. A critic exercises the build, puts it beside Bridgefy with the labels stripped, says which is better and names the single biggest remaining gap, then hands it back. The loop runs until the critic picks ours.

Every number, and where it came from

Per-hop distance in a built-up area and end-to-end delivery across three or more hops are the two the project is graded on. A blank is a number nobody has produced yet, not a zero. Nothing here is a field measurement until it says so: this machine has no radios and no phones, so hardware numbers come from the field rounds and everything else is modelled.
